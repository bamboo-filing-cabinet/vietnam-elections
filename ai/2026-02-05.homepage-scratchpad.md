# Homepage scratchpad (2026-02-05)

## Context
- Goal: reassess the vietnam-elections homepage and decide whether to simplify the primary actions.
- Updated signal: the 2026 National Assembly election is next month and is major news.
- Implication: 2026 should likely be the primary action, not labeled “coming soon.”
- Open question: is the current homepage already good for other reasons?

## Quick take (updated)
- The homepage should match the current news cycle: 2026 is the primary destination, with 2025 as a secondary “previous cycle.”
- Two CTAs could be simpler and clearer, but might hide other valuable entry points if those are genuinely popular or distinct.
- The strongest homepage typically answers three things fast: What is this? What can I do now? Why should I trust it?

## What to evaluate on the existing homepage
- Primary task clarity: can a first-time visitor immediately pick the right action?
- Readiness signaling: are unfinished parts labeled as such without feeling like dead ends?
- Scope & trust: does the page convey what data/timeframe it covers and why it is reliable?
- Navigation depth: do we force users into a cycle view even if they want a quick fact or a summary?

## Possible directions (not decisions)
1. **Two-button focus (2026 primary)**
   - Make 2026 the primary action; 2025 a secondary “previous cycle”.
   - Keep other items as supporting links (footer/section) so they remain discoverable.

2. **Hybrid: two main CTAs + “Explore more”**
   - Primary buttons for 2025/2026.
   - Secondary list of other features or data views.

3. **Narrative-first**
   - Short intro: what the site is, what’s inside, who it’s for.
   - Then two CTAs; then quick stats or highlights to build trust.

## Questions to answer before changing anything
- What are the four actions today (exact labels, destinations)?
- Which are actually used or most important for the core audience?
- Are any of the four actions redundant with the 2025 cycle page?
- Do we need a “quick stats” or “overview” action for people not ready to dive into a cycle?
- If 2026 is next month, what data is ready now vs. expected closer to election day?

## Audit plan
- Capture current homepage layout, copy, and CTAs.
- Identify user journeys: first-time visitor vs. returning researcher.
- Map each action to a user job; eliminate overlap or weak utility.

## Current homepage audit (from live page + source)
- Primary CTAs (4):
  - Search candidates (2021) → /elections/na15-2021/candidates
  - Browse constituencies (2021) → /elections/na15-2021/constituencies
  - Sources & methodology → /sources
  - 2026 cycle (coming soon) → /elections/na16-2026
- Secondary sections:
  - “What you can do” repeats the 2021 tasks without links.
  - “Quick links” duplicates the 2021 actions + Sources/Methodology.
- Tension with current news cycle:
  - 2026 is visually de-emphasized (dashed, muted, “coming soon”) even though it should be primary.
  - 2021 actions dominate the main CTA row.

## Initial recommendations (based on audit)
- Make 2026 the primary CTA with “live” or “in progress” framing.
- Demote 2021 to a secondary CTA (e.g., “Previous cycle: 2021”).
- Move Sources/Methodology to a secondary row or quick links.
- Adjust “What you can do” to reflect 2026 availability instead of 2021-only tasks.

## Provisional recommendation (pending audit)
- Make 2026 the primary CTA and lead story on the page.
- Keep 2025 as a clear secondary option (previous cycle / archive).
- Preserve other actions as smaller links if they provide unique value.
- Strengthen the intro text to clarify scope, credibility, and what’s available now.
